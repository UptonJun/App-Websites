Support

Help with ShortGB, the local-first script and teleprompter tool for short-drama actors and short-video creators. Most questions are answered below.

Contact us

Report a bug, ask a question, or request a feature by email:

contact@shortgb.com

Do not attach unpublished scripts or confidential production material unless it is necessary to explain the issue. For privacy details, see the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. ShortGB keeps scripts, characters, reading settings, and prompt positions in a local database and local app preferences inside the app’s sandbox. It does not upload them to a server. There is no account and no cloud sync.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. ShortGB has no sign-in, subscription, or account system, and it runs fully offline. Scripts never need to be uploaded to use the app.

Is my data synced between devices?

No. ShortGB does not provide account-based or cloud sync. Data entered on one device is not automatically available on another device. To move scripts, export them as .shortgb files and import them on the other device.

How do I back up or move my scripts?

From More on the home screen, choose the option to export all scripts. ShortGB creates one folder containing a .shortgb file for each script and opens the system document picker so you can save it to Files or another location you trust.

To export a single script, open the script editor and use its export action. To restore, choose the import option and select a TXT, RTF, text-based PDF, or .shortgb file. Export backups regularly, especially before changing devices or reinstalling the app.

What file types can I import?

ShortGB supports TXT, RTF, text-based PDF, and .shortgb. Scanned PDFs have no selectable text, so V1 does not support OCR or scanned PDFs. For best results, use a text-based PDF or export the script as plain text.

Why doesn’t the sample script come back after I delete it?

The built-in sample script is imported only once, on first launch. If you delete it, ShortGB remembers that it was already imported and does not restore it automatically. You can still create or import your own scripts.

Why is the play button disabled?

The play button is disabled when the current scene fits on one screen or has no content, because there is nothing to scroll. Add more lines or choose a scene with content.

How does prompting work?

Select a character to highlight your lines, then start the prompt. A 3-2-1 countdown appears before playback by default and can be turned off in Reading Settings. Tap the text during playback to pause or resume. While paused, you can drag the text, drag the progress bar, use −5s / +5s, or jump to the previous or next line.

The speed control supports fine adjustment, ±5 steps, an exact value, and common presets. Time remaining is estimated at the current speed. The eye button shows only your lines with nearby context.

Why are there only Regular, Bold, and Light font weights?

ShortGB uses system font weights so the app stays fast and ships no third-party font licenses. Text size, line spacing, paragraph spacing, reading width, text color, and background color can be adjusted in Reading Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual scripts in the app, or delete ShortGB from your device to remove its local app data. Exported .shortgb files are separate copies outside the app and are not deleted with it, so manage or remove them yourself. Because ShortGB does not create a cloud copy of your scripts, the developer cannot restore deleted data.

The app shows a recovery screen or a database check failed. What should I do?

Export a backup first if the recovery screen offers that option, then follow the on-screen steps to rebuild the database. If the problem continues, contact us with your device model, iOS version, ShortGB version, and the steps that led to the message.

Does ShortGB collect analytics or track me?

No. ShortGB has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK collection, and it does not send script content to a server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a security issue. How should I report it?

Email contact@shortgb.com with a description of the problem and the steps needed to reproduce it. Do not include unpublished scripts, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Before you contact us

Including the following helps us investigate:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version;
  • the ShortGB version shown in the app;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • the steps that reproduce the issue, if it is repeatable.

Do not attach your full script library, confidential production material, or exported backups unless we specifically ask for a minimal sample.
