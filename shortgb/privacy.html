Privacy Policy

ShortGB is designed as a fully local script and prompt tool. Your scripts and reading settings stay on your device, and ShortGB does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

Effective date: November 24, 2026 · Last updated: November 24, 2026

The short version

  • ShortGB does not require an account, sign-in, or registration.
  • Your scripts, characters, and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • ShortGB does not upload script content or personal data to a server.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK collection in ShortGB.
  • ShortGB reads or writes only files you explicitly choose to import or export.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the ShortGB iOS app (“ShortGB”, “the app”) handles information. It applies to the app on iPhone and iPad. It does not apply to this website’s hosting provider or to third-party services you choose to use outside the app.

2. Information stored on your device

ShortGB stores the information you enter so that the app can work:

  • scripts, including episode and scene structure;
  • characters, dialogue lines, and action lines you create or import;
  • the character currently selected for prompting;
  • reading settings such as font weight, text size, spacing, reading width, colors, countdown, and reading guide;
  • prompt position and other app settings;
  • a record that the built-in sample script has already been imported.

This information is stored in the app’s local sandbox on your device, including a local SQLite database and app preferences. It is not sent to ShortGB’s developer, because ShortGB has no server that receives script data.

3. No collection or transmission to us

ShortGB does not operate a backend for your scripts. The app does not transmit your scripts, characters, lines, settings, or prompt positions to the developer or to third parties. ShortGB does not include analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

ShortGB does not create a user profile, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Import, export, and file access

ShortGB reads or writes only files you explicitly choose to import or export. It does not scan other files on your device.

You can import TXT, RTF, text-based PDF, and .shortgb files. You can export a single .shortgb file or export all scripts as a folder of .shortgb files. Import and export use Apple’s system document picker and share sheet so that you choose the file and destination.

If you send an exported file to another app, person, or cloud service, that destination is outside ShortGB and is governed by its own privacy practices. ShortGB does not upload the file on your behalf.

5. Device backups

ShortGB does not provide cloud sync or its own backup service. Whether app data is included in an iCloud or computer backup depends on your system backup settings and Apple’s backup behavior. Exporting a .shortgb file is the most direct way to keep a copy of your scripts under your own control.

6. Retention and deletion

Your scripts and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Deleting a script removes it from the app’s database. Deleting ShortGB removes its local app data, subject to any copies included in a device backup.

Exported files are separate copies outside the app. Deleting the app does not delete files you exported to Files or another destination; manage those copies yourself. Because ShortGB does not receive your script data, there is no server-side copy for the developer to access or delete.

7. Children’s privacy

ShortGB is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app collects no personal information and has no social, upload, or public posting features.

8. This website

This website provides support and legal information for ShortGB. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when you visit the site, under its own privacy policy. The ShortGB app itself does not contact this website.

9. Changes to this policy

If ShortGB’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change. If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, or another feature that processes data, the policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will provide clear notice and consent options where required.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@shortgb.com. Please do not include unpublished scripts or confidential production material unless it is necessary to explain your question.
