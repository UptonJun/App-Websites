CentsCove

A private, local-first expense ledger for iPhone and iPad. Formerly Ordio.

Privacy at a glance

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Ledger data is stored only on your device.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • No bank account or payment card details are requested.
  • Face ID and Touch ID are handled entirely by iOS.
