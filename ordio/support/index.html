Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. CentsCove keeps your ledger in a local database inside the app’s storage and does not upload it to a server. There is no account and no cloud sync.

Is my data synced between devices?

No. CentsCove does not provide account-based or cloud sync. Data entered on one device is not automatically available on another device.

How do I back up or move my records?

Use Settings → Export CSV. CentsCove creates a CSV file containing your records and opens the iOS share sheet so you can save it to Files or send it to another location you trust.

To restore, use the import option in Settings and choose a CSV file. Import replaces all current records in the app, so export a backup first if you may need the existing data.

Does CentsCove connect to my bank or read my card details?

No. CentsCove is a manual expense ledger. It does not connect to banks, does not request bank account or payment card credentials, and does not read messages or notifications to detect transactions.

How does the privacy lock work?

When enabled, CentsCove asks iOS to authenticate you with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. iOS performs the check and returns only the result. CentsCove does not read or store biometric data.

If Face ID or Touch ID is unavailable or not enrolled, iOS may offer the device passcode. Make sure a device passcode is set in iOS Settings.

Face ID is not working. What should I check?

Confirm that Face ID is set up in iOS Settings → Face ID & Passcode, that the app has permission to use it, and that your device supports the feature. Try locking and unlocking the device, then reopen CentsCove. If the issue continues, contact us with your iOS version and the steps you took.

Why is the widget not updating?

The widget shows an on-device summary generated by the app. Open CentsCove after changing records so the summary can refresh, then check the widget again. Widget refresh timing is also controlled by iOS.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records or categories in the app; those records are removed from the local ledger. Deleting CentsCove from your device removes its local app data container. CSV files you exported earlier are separate copies outside the app and are not deleted with it, so manage or remove them yourself. Because CentsCove does not create a cloud copy of your ledger, the developer cannot restore deleted records.

Can I use more than one currency?

Yes. CentsCove supports multiple currencies and keeps amounts in their original currency. It does not fetch exchange rates or convert records using a live rate.

I found a security issue. How should I report it?

Send an email to workios10@163.com describing the problem and any information needed to reproduce it. Do not include personal financial records, passwords, or other unnecessary sensitive information in your email.

Before you contact us

Including the following helps us investigate:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version;
  • the CentsCove version shown in the App Store or in the app;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • the steps that reproduce the issue, if it is repeatable.

Do not attach your exported ledger, screenshots containing financial details, or any account credentials.
