Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

On your device. Ordio keeps your ledger in a local database inside the app’s storage and does not upload it to a server. There is no account and no cloud sync.

Is my data synced between devices?

No. Ordio does not provide account-based or cloud sync. Data entered on one device is not automatically available on another device.

How do I back up or move my records?

Use Settings → Export CSV. Ordio creates a CSV file containing your records and opens the iOS share sheet so you can save it to Files or send it to another location you trust.

To restore, use the import option in Settings and choose a CSV file. Import replaces all current records in the app, so export a backup first if you may need the existing data.

Does Ordio connect to my bank or read my card details?

No. Ordio is a manual expense ledger. It does not connect to banks, does not request bank account or payment card credentials, and does not read messages or notifications to detect transactions.

How does the privacy lock work?

When enabled, Ordio asks iOS to authenticate you with Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. iOS performs the check and returns only the result. Ordio does not read or store biometric data.

If Face ID or Touch ID is unavailable or not enrolled, iOS may offer the device passcode. Make sure a device passcode is set in iOS Settings.

Face ID is not working. What should I check?

Confirm that Face ID is set up in iOS Settings → Face ID & Passcode, that the app has permission to use it, and that your device supports the feature. Try locking and unlocking the device, then reopen Ordio. If the issue continues, contact us with your iOS version and the steps you took.

Why is the widget not updating?

The widget shows an on-device summary generated by the app. Open Ordio after changing records so the summary can refresh, then check the widget again. Widget refresh timing is also controlled by iOS.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual records or categories in the app. To remove all local app data, delete Ordio from your device. If you enabled device backups, iOS may retain a copy according to your backup settings; see the Privacy Policy.

Can I use more than one currency?

Yes. Ordio supports multiple currencies and keeps amounts in their original currency. It does not fetch exchange rates or convert records using a live rate.

I found a security issue. How should I report it?

Please avoid posting exploitable details publicly. Open a minimal issue asking for a private contact channel, without including personal data or step-by-step attack details.

Before you contact us

Including the following helps us investigate:

  • your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version;
  • the Ordio version shown in the App Store or in the app;
  • what you expected to happen and what happened instead;
  • the steps that reproduce the issue, if it is repeatable.

Do not attach your exported ledger, screenshots containing financial details, or any account credentials.
