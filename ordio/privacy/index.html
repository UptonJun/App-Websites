Privacy Policy

Ordio is designed as a local-first expense ledger. Your records stay on your device, and Ordio does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

Effective date: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

The short version

  • Ordio does not require an account.
  • Your ledger is stored locally on your device.
  • Ordio does not upload your ledger or personal data to a server.
  • Ordio does not collect bank card, bank account, or other payment credentials.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, and device passcode verification are performed by iOS; Ordio never receives biometric data.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDK collection in Ordio.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Ordio iOS app (“Ordio”, “the app”) handles information. It applies to the app and its widget. It does not apply to this website’s hosting provider or to third-party services you choose to use outside the app.

2. Information stored on your device

Ordio stores the information you enter so that the app can work:

  • expense records, including amounts, currencies, dates, notes, and categories;
  • categories you create and related display preferences;
  • recurring expense rules and their status;
  • app settings, including your currency choices and whether the privacy lock is enabled;
  • a small widget summary (for example, this month’s total and top category) shared with the Ordio widget through Apple’s App Group container on the same device.

This information is stored in the app’s local container on your device. It is not sent to Ordio’s developer, because Ordio has no server that receives ledger data.

3. No collection or transmission to us

Ordio does not operate a backend for your ledger. The app does not transmit your records, notes, categories, settings, or widget summary to the developer or to third parties. Ordio does not include analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

4. No bank or payment account access

Ordio is a manual expense ledger. It does not connect to banks, does not request bank account numbers, bank card numbers, card security codes, or online banking credentials, and does not read messages, emails, or notifications to infer transactions. Any payment card or bank information you choose to type into a note is your own content and remains on your device like any other note.

5. Face ID, Touch ID, and device passcode

If you enable the privacy lock, Ordio asks iOS to authenticate you through Apple’s LocalAuthentication framework using Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode. iOS performs the authentication and returns only a success, cancel, or failure result to the app.

Ordio does not access, read, store, or transmit Face ID data, Touch ID data, facial images, fingerprint data, or biometric templates. Biometric information is managed by iOS and protected by your device. Ordio only stores your preference for whether the privacy lock is enabled.

6. Export, import, and sharing

Ordio lets you export your records as a CSV file and import a CSV backup. These actions happen only when you start them. Export creates a file in the app’s temporary storage and hands it to the iOS share sheet so you can choose a destination. Import uses the iOS document picker so you can choose a file.

If you send an exported file to another app, person, or cloud service, that destination is outside Ordio and is governed by its own privacy practices. Ordio does not upload the file on your behalf.

7. Widget

The Ordio widget reads the on-device summary stored in the shared App Group container. The summary is generated locally. It is not sent to a server. Widgets are rendered by iOS on your device.

8. Device backups

If you enable iCloud Backup or other device backups, iOS may include Ordio’s local app data in your device backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple and are subject to Apple’s terms and privacy policy. Ordio does not create or receive a separate copy of your data.

9. Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its local app container, including the ledger database and widget summary, subject to any device backup you have enabled. Because Ordio does not receive your ledger data, there is no server-side copy for the developer to delete.

10. Your choices and rights

You control the data on your device. You can edit or delete individual records and categories in the app, disable the privacy lock, export a copy of your data, or delete the app and its data. Ordio does not sell or share personal data, and it does not collect personal data for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking.

If you have a question about privacy, contact us using the support channel below. Because we do not hold a server copy of your ledger, we generally cannot access or restore your local records.

11. Children’s privacy

Ordio is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app does not collect personal information at all.

12. This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs when you visit the site, under GitHub’s privacy statement. The Ordio app itself does not contact this website.

13. Changes to this policy

If Ordio’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change. Material changes will be reflected on this page before they apply to a released version of the app.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/UptonJun/App-Websites/issues. Please do not include personal financial information, receipts, or account details in a public issue.
